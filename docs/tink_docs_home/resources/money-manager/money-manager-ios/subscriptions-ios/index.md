## Overview[](#overview)

The Subscriptions tool is designed to help you, and your end-users, get a clear understanding of your active subscriptions and their associated costs on a monthly, quarterly, and annual basis.

## Displaying Subscriptions[](#displaying-subscriptions)

To display a Subscriptions list, simply instantiate a `SubscriptionsViewController`.

```
let subscriptionsViewController = SubscriptionsViewController()
show(subscriptionsViewController, sender: self)
```
