In today's dynamic financial landscape, staying on top of your investments and pension plans has never been more critical. However, businesses face a challenge of gaining a comprehensive understanding of their clients investments and pension data. Traditionally, this is the result of lack of accessible data beyond the confines of individual financial institutions. Tink’s Investment product is designed to bridge these gaps by harnessing the power of Open Banking.

## Challenges when lacking real-time investment information[](#challenges-when-lacking-real-time-investment-information)

-   Providing tailored advice: understanding your clients investments is the cornerstone of delivering exceptional financial services. Without timely access to this crucial data, it can inhibit your ability to offer tailored solutions and valuable advice
-   No consolidated wealth overview for users: many individuals face challenges getting a holistic overview of their investments as it often requires piecing together a complex puzzle with missing fragments. This can lead to challenges in finding appropriate investment strategies
-   High customer acquisition cost: Moving or aggregating investment accounts can be a long manual process for both the end-user and the customer. The complexity can lead to friction and lock-in effects, posing a big challenge for attracting new customers on to your platform

## How we solve these challenges[](#how-we-solve-these-challenges)

-   Tink’s Investment product enables you to fetch investment and holding data from financial institutions in Spain and Sweden through a single API in real time
-   The product can be integrated seamlessly within your customer journey through an SDK that can be customized to match your user interface
-   The SDK allows your users to securely select and connect their investment account from their bank or platform in order to fetch real-time investment and pension data in less than a minute
-   Once the user has connected his bank account(s), Tink fetches the investment and pension account data from the bank's API and returns it in a standardized format via the Investment API

## Use cases with Investments[](#use-cases-with-investments)

**Holistic view on users investments**: The Investments Product enables you to consolidate all your clients investment data into a single, coherent interface. This allows you to provide your clients a comprehensive, holistic view of your clients' financial positions across providers.

**Streamline account transfers**: Historically, transferring assets between financial institutions has been a labyrinthine process, fraught with inefficiencies and complications, deterring clients from exploring better investment opportunities. Investments removes these roadblocks, enabling you to facilitate a seamless transfer of investments by validating users existing investments in under a minute.

**Personalise wealth advisory**: Understanding your clients investments is the cornerstone of delivering exceptional financial services. Without timely access to this crucial data, your ability to provide personalized recommendations, optimize product offerings, and nurture strong client relationships is severely compromised. With Investments, you can drive personalisation and automation of wealth advisory with a holistic understanding of your client’s net worth across providers.
