Analytics is used to track key performance integration metrics of your integration. Use the results to make data-driven decisions, track where users drop off, and monitor individual bank connections.

To access Analytics:

1.  Log in to Console.
2.  Select the product for which you want to see analytics.
3.  Select the **Analytics** tab.

## Data points[](#data-points)

All products currently show the following data points:

-   **Completion rate**: the percentage of users who have completed the Tink flow, measured or from when a user has started the authentication process
-   **Reports generated**: the number of reports that have been generated
-   **Flow breakdown**: a breakdown of the next-step conversion rate for each step in the Tink flow
-   **Flow breakdown by financial institution**: a breakdown of the next-step conversion rate for each step in the Tink flow by financial institution

For **Payment Initiation**, we provide additional data points:

-   **Payments initiated**/**Volumes initiated**: the number of successfully initiated payments and the total volume of successfully initiated payments, in euros.
-   **Errors per financial institution**: a breakdown of errors per financial institution.
-   **Top causes of payment errors**: a list of the top reasons for why a payment initiation was declined or incomplete.

## FAQ[](#faq)

**How often is data updated?**

Once per hour.

**What are payment errors?**

Payment errors are based on responses that we get from individual banks when a payment has failed or was incomplete.
