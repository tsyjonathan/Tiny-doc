The urgency for gaining comprehensive, fast and reliable insights into your clients loan data is higher than ever before. High inflation rates pressuring consumers ability to keep up with cost of living and financial obligations. Lenders, on the other hand face the challenge of handling an increasing share of customers running into arrears or seeking repayment adjustments and debt restructuring. However, businesses encounter a hurdle when it comes to accessing timely and relevant information about their clients loans. This challenge stems from the lack of accessible data beyond the boundaries of individual financial institutions. Tink's Loan product is here to bridge this gap by harnessing the power of Open Banking.

## Challenges when lacking real-time loans information[](#challenges-when-lacking-real-time-loans-information)

-   Tailored financial advice: Having a holistic view of your clients finances is the cornerstone of delivering exceptional financial services. Understanding a user's current debt is an essential part of this. Without timely access to current liabilities, it can inhibit your ability to offer tailored solutions and valuable advice. In the realm of loans, missing insights can lead to ineffective loan management, interest rate optimization, and risk assessment.
-   Fragmented loan Information: Many individuals face challenges when trying to obtain a holistic overview of their loans, as it often requires piecing together a complex puzzle with missing fragments. This fragmented view can lead to challenges in identifying suitable loan strategies, refinancing opportunities, and debt consolidation.
-   High customer acquisition cost: Moving or consolidating loans can be a labor-intensive and often manual process for both the end-user and the financial institution. The complexity and friction associated with these processes can lead to significant lock-in effects, posing a substantial challenge for attracting new clients to your platform.

## How we solve these challenges[](#how-we-solve-these-challenges)

Tink's Loan product empowers your business by providing access to real-time loan data from various financial institutions, offering a comprehensive view of your clients' loan portfolios. Here's how we tackle these challenges:

-   Real-Time Loan Data Access: Tink’s Loans product enables you to fetch loan information from financial institutions through a single API in real-time. This means you can stay up to date with your clients loan data, helping you make informed decisions and offer personalized services
-   Seamless Integration: The product can be integrated seamlessly into your customer journey through an SDK that can be customized to match your user interface. This ensures that the access to loan data feels like a native part of your platform, enhancing the user experience.
-   User-Friendly loan connection: The SDK allows your users to securely select and connect their loan accounts from their bank or platform, facilitating the retrieval of real-time loan data in less than a minute.
-   Standardized Loan Data: Once the user has connected their bank account(s), Tink fetches the loan data from the bank's API and returns it in a standardized format via the Loans API. This standardization simplifies data analysis, risk assessment, and reporting, allowing you to make more informed decisions.

## Use cases with Loans[](#use-cases-with-loans)

**Loan portfolio overview** The Loans Product enables you to consolidate all your clients loans data into a single, coherent interface. This allows you to provide your clients a comprehensive, holistic view of your clients' outstanding balances, interest rates, repayment schedules and more to help them understand their financial obligations.

**Refinancing opportunities** By analysing loans data in real time, you can identify opportunities for refinancing instantly. If a user is eligible for a loan with a lower interest rate, you can recommend refinancing options to help your clients save money

**Financial education** Educational resources and content can be customised based on a user’s loan portfolio. You can offer articles, videos or webinars tailored to their specific financial situation
