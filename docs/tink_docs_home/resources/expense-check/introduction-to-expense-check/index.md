Businesses require an accurate picture of an individual's expense streams, for example, to assess loan applications. Most companies rely on a combination of two ways to assess affordability: basic statistical estimates and user-provided material.

![expense-check-comparison-of-methods](https://images.ctfassets.net/tmqu5vj33f7w/4umCxGrOzUDchucj97Luhe/1a32e71a714d4e2c44f8b08b32b0dffd/expense-check-comparison-of-methods.jpg)

Significant problems are found in the estimates and materials: estimates may be good for a population as a whole but risk being wrong on an individual level. Reviewing bank statements is a slow, fraud-prone, and tedious process.

With Expense Check, Tink solves these problems by combining robust and reliable open-banking power with sophisticated data science and easily integrated software. An individual's spending is presented across several categories, which results in accurate outcome that can cater to regulatory creditworthiness guidelines.

## Easy integration[](#easy-integration)

We deliver Expense Check through our [SDK](/Tiny-doc/tink_docs_home/resources/expense-check/setup-and-integrate-expense-check/), which uses a single line of code for you to integrate your product with our platform.

When an end user has consented to share their data and authenticated to their bank, an automated JSON report is created for you in less than a minute.

![expense-check-flow-SE](https://images.ctfassets.net/tmqu5vj33f7w/29iSGDX1wArtAdCaUptQIx/e3bcccf7f145e32fd9a051ed81ebbfce/expense-check-flow-SE.gif)

Tink handles the complexities involved with connecting with thousands of banks across Europe, including all connection and authentication methods that risk being changed at any time.
