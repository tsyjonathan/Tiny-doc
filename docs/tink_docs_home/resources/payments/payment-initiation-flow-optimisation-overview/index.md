## Branding[](#branding)

You can customise the look and feel of your Payments flow by using your brand’s preferred colours and fonts. For more information, see this [guide](/Tiny-doc/tink_docs_home/resources/tink-link-web/tink-link-web-customization/). Note that to customise fonts you’ll need to contact our support team.

*Image removed: Flow optimization - theme (1)*

Please note: when adjusting the colours and fonts you need to ensure the legal notices are clearly visible for all users.

### Logo[](#logo)

Your logo must be a PNG image, 64 pixels in height and a maximum of 400 pixels in width. Please make sure the logo works well with your theme’s _Content background_ color.

To upload a new logo or change an existing one, open Console, go to **\[your\_app\]** > **App settings** > **Customise** and select **Customise your theme**.
