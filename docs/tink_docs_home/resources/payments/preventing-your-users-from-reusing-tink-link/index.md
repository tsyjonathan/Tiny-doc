You might want to use a Tink URL over and over, without any kind of expiration. Some may want to prevent Tink URLs from being used more than once.

We offer configuration options to prevent Tink URLs from being used twice. When these options are enabled, Tink Link presents additional screens when payment is retried.

*Image removed: Preventing your users from reusing Tink Link-3*

Consider using this feature if one of these scenarios apply to you:

-   You need a one-to-one mapping between a payment and its initiation journey.
-   In addition to your own checks, you want to further minimise the risk of double payment.
-   Your end users must perform at least one additional action on your system before they are allowed to retry payment.

Please contact your customer representative if you would like to use these configuration options.
