## Introduction[](#introduction)

For a successful payment flow, businesses need to verify that a customer has sufficient funds to cover the payment. For direct debit payments, merchants lack visibility to what is their customers spending power before attempting to draw funds from their accounts. This can open businesses to risks of fraud and chargeback fees, resulting in lost revenues and a negative cash flow. For end users, they have the risk of receiving a penalty fee for failed direct debits in addition to having a negative impact on their credit score.

Main risks when lacking real-time balance information for direct debit

-   Insufficient funds is the #1 source of failed direct debit payments, causing businesses to be subject to fraud and incur chargebacks. This ultimately leads to lost revenues and negative cash flow.
-   End users may receive a penalty fee for failed direct debits, in addition to potential negative affects on credit rating. This can result in a negative customer experiences associated with your brand.
-   Reclaiming failed payments leads to extra back-office workload, wasting time and resources.

### Our solution[](#our-solution)

With Balance check, merchants are able to verify balances in real time within seconds, with data straight from a user’s bank account. Empowering merchants with balance data, it improves conversion and reduces the risk of non-payment and churn with a faster and easier onboarding and payment setup process for your business and your new customers.

Tink provides an automated, machine readable report with the account information necessary to set up the direct debit mandate. With Balance Check, merchants have the ability to ping the users’ balance anytime for up-to 90 days with an active consent, to understand the spending power.

What this means for merchants:

-   Increase your collection rate with smarter billing schedules and the ability to give users advance notice of insufficient funds.
-   Improved risk protection against payment fraud with secure, real-time balance data directly from the users’ bank account.
-   Fewer resources spent on monitoring direct debits and chasing failed payments.
