Tink Link is our front-end web SDK for account authentication and payment initiation. *Image removed: Tink Link Web*

AIS and PIS are two main services enabled by open banking, both requiring user authentication and authorization. This is why we developed Tink Link, our front-end SDK for account authentication and payment initiation. Users simply choose their provider and are automatically directed to the required authentication or payment flow, whether that be a web redirect, app redirect, or handled directly within the Tink Link flow.

Please refer to our product page for more information about Tink Link.
