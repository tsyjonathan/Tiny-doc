## Brief summary[](#brief-summary)

The Open Banking UK legislation was set up by the Competition and Market Authority (CMA) on behalf of the UK government to enhance competition and innovation in the financial services. The regulation came into force on the 13th of January 2018, preceding [PSD2](/Tiny-doc/tink_docs_home/resources/aggregation/open-banking-glossary/) by over a year, and requires the 9 largest banks (referred to as CMA9) to provide third-party providers the ability to access payment accounts and initiate payments on behalf of the customers.

The CMA9 must adhere to the framework developed by the Open Banking Implementation Entity ([OBIE](/Tiny-doc/tink_docs_home/resources/aggregation/open-banking-glossary/)), which was set up in 2016 to deliver Open Banking. This framework includes technical, user-experience and operational guidelines, including a design specification for APIs and security standards.
