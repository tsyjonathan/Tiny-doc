## Brief summary[](#brief-summary)

The [Open Banking UK legislation](https://www.openbanking.org.uk/) was set up by the [Competition and Market Authority](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/competition-and-markets-authority) (CMA) on behalf of the UK government to enhance competition and innovation in the financial services. The regulation came into force on the 13th of January 2018, preceding [PSD2](https://docs.tink.com/resources/aggregation/open-banking-glossary) by over a year, and requires the 9 largest banks (referred to as CMA9) to provide third-party providers the ability to access payment accounts and initiate payments on behalf of the customers.

The CMA9 must adhere to the framework developed by the Open Banking Implementation Entity ([OBIE](https://docs.tink.com/resources/aggregation/open-banking-glossary)), which was set up in 2016 to deliver Open Banking. This framework includes technical, user-experience and operational guidelines, including a design specification for APIs and security standards.
