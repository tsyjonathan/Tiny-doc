It's vital to quickly onboard customers for short time-to-market. Turn a prospect into a paying customer and immediately provide them with value from your product. This is especially important for SMBs and sole traders who don't have time and resources to spend on lengthy onboarding processes.

There are many challenges that can slow down a account-verification process:

-   Slow customer-onboarding processes that risk leading to drop-offs
-   Error handling due to erroneously filled-out paperwork
-   Various levels of risk, including fraud and sending money to the wrong bank account
-   Dealing with businesses in various markets and with different complexities

The operational costs for continuously dealing with these challenges tend to grow high, especially at scale.

## How we solve these problems[](#how-we-solve-these-problems)

By using Business Account Check, Tink allows you to securely connect to a business bank account and fetch real-time data. This enables you to verify account ownership in less than a minute.

As data is fetched directly from a bank in real-time, you are protected against fraud. Rely on data that's both accurate and up-to-date.

Business Account Check is completely digital, which means we entirely remove the human-error factor and automate the account-verification process.

There are thousands of banks across Europe with their own connections and authentication methods that can be updated at any time. We continuously handle all of those so that you won't have to.

## Integration, customization, and result[](#integration-customization-and-result)

Our open-banking powered API allows you to seamlessly integrate our technology with your customer journey. This is done through an SDK that results in a single line of code that you can offer to potential customers.

Feel free to customize not only the features that you can offer your users, but the entire user journey to truly make it seamless. We provide a variety of customization options, from simplifying customer choices to branding your UI theme to match your company specifications.

At the end of a verification journey, you receive a JSON-formatted report that contains verified account information that allows for automated and scalable account-verification processes.
