Data Enrichment serves as the data processing layer for you to turn your customers' financial transactions into information sources. Access transactional data from all of your customers' bank accounts and build value-added services on top of cleaned, enriched, and up-to-date data. This lets you build engaging experiences, improved efficiencies, and increased retention to meet customer needs and expectations.

One integration with Tink is all you need to connect with thousands of banks and fetch up-to-date and enriched financial data. Use the data to create and offer insights.

## Capabilities[](#capabilities)

Data Enrichment is packaged as a modular set of capabilities. This allows you to pick and choose the capabilities that you want to implement. Use the following capabilities to suit your use cases, for example, contract management, liquidity/balance management, and loyalty/usage of bank app/web environments.

## Categorisation[](#categorisation)

Make sense of your customers’ financial data by receiving cleaned transaction descriptions and categorisation of all transactions. Tink applies a machine-learning approach to categorisation that enables a clear and easily digestible financial overview based on numerous data points.

## Recurring transactions[](#recurring-transactions)

Help your customers better understand their fixed cash flow with recurring transactions. Give them information on their subscriptions, utility bills, loans and transfers, and create features that enables a good understanding of their finances and what they can do about it.

*Image removed: Recurring transactions*

## Predicted recurring transactions[](#predicted-recurring-transactions)

Provide your customers with a forecast of their fixed commitments and cash flow, enabling them to prevent overspend. Supply a future 30-day overview that includes charges that aren't usually announced ahead of time such as card payments.

*Image removed: Predicted recurring transactions*

## Eco-system partners[](#eco-system-partners)

Easily connect with third-party providers, such as carbon footprint trackers or subscription managers, to enrich your customers’ transactional data beyond financial attributes and actions while delivering innovative and engaging solutions in your digital banking app.
